About

Carbon numbers, and the tools
that make them repeatable.

I am an early-career engineer working across two connected areas. The first is building sustainability: embodied carbon, life cycle assessment and building performance. The second is computational and parametric design: Rhino, Grasshopper, Python automation and BIM. One tells me what a building costs the climate. The other lets me test that answer across many options instead of one.

My MASc research at the University of Toronto, supervised by Prof. Shoshanna Saxe, measured material use and embodied carbon across building refurbishment scenarios. The workflow ran from a multi-phase Revit model through material take-off to cradle-to-gate LCA. The raw take-off came out at roughly 16,000 rows. A Python pipeline reduced that to 4,522 structured line items across 25 building-phase scenarios, so scenarios could be compared consistently instead of rebuilt by hand.

That research is published as a first-author paper in Energy and Buildings (vol. 370, 2026) and was presented at SBE25 at ETH Zurich. Alongside it I work in Grasshopper on parametric studies, including PV array placement and algorithmic building geometry. I hold LEED Green Associate and the One Click LCA Construction LCA Specialist certification.

🏛 Featured · MASc Thesis

Embodied Carbon Across Building Refurbishment Scenarios

MASc research at the University of Toronto, supervised by Prof. Shoshanna Saxe, covering five real refurbishment, renovation and adaptive reuse projects in Canada and the United States. I built multi-phase building models in Revit and used material take-off to convert modelled volumes into material masses, then into cradle-to-gate embodied carbon. The take-off produced roughly 16,000 raw rows. A Python pipeline cleaned, mapped and aggregated those into 4,522 structured line items across 25 building-phase scenarios, which turned a manual, error-prone comparison into one that could be rerun whenever the model or the emission factors changed. The retained structures kept 60 to 98 percent of their original structural mass, while embodied GHG still rose 1 to 60 percent against the originals, driven by new insulation, windows and concrete. Published as first author in Energy and Buildings, vol. 370 (2026).

📐 Featured · Computational Design

Parametric Design Studies in Grasshopper

Parametric definitions built in Rhino and Grasshopper, covering PV array placement across building surfaces and algorithmic generation of building geometry. The design variables are exposed as parameters, so panel layouts and massing options can be generated and compared as a set rather than drawn one at a time. Python handles the data preparation and exports the results for analysis, which keeps the geometry work and the performance numbers in the same loop.

🏆 Winner · Climate Impacts Hackathon 2024

Toronto Urban Heat Vulnerability & Cooling Centre Siting

Led a Python and QGIS pipeline to identify cooling centre locations for Toronto. Downscaled CanESM5 climate projections (SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5, 2070 to 2100) using Quantile Delta Mapping, calculated Humidex heat hazard days, and overlaid population vulnerability indicators to produce ward-level risk maps. First place.

🌱 Comparative LCA

Cradle-to-Grave LCA of Three Dietary Patterns

Full cradle-to-grave life cycle assessment of Canadian, Mediterranean and EAT-Lancet diets in OpenLCA, using ecoinvent v3.10, the IPCC 2013 impact method and the AWARE water scarcity model. Quantified greenhouse gas and freshwater stress impacts, ran multi-parameter sensitivity analysis, and scaled the results to estimate national reductions from dietary shifts.

OpenLCA ecoinvent v3.10 IPCC 2013 AWARE Sensitivity Analysis
