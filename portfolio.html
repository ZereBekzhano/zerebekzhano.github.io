About

Where data meets
decarbonisation strategy.

I'm a civil and building engineer from Kazakhstan with a graduate research specialisation in material use in building renovation and embodied GHG assessment. My thesis work, published through the University of Toronto, quantifies material intensity and GHG emissions across refurbishment scenarios — combining Revit-based material take-off workflows, LCA methodology, and data analysis in Python.

From Nazarbayev University to Politecnico di Milano to the University of Toronto's Centre for the Sustainable Built Environment (CSBE), my career has been shaped by the belief that climate action demands both rigorous quantification and scalable strategy. I've published in Q1 journals during undergraduate, co-authored a conference paper at ETH Zurich SBE25, and led the team that won the Climate Impacts Hackathon 2024.

What distinguishes me is breadth matched with depth: I work across engineering, data analysis, and sustainability assessment — and bring multilingual communication skills across four languages. I thrive in multidisciplinary environments where analytical rigour and systems thinking are both essential.

🏛 ⭐ Graduate Thesis · Flagship
Material Use in Renovation, Retrofit and Refurbishment of Existing Buildings
MAS thesis research at the University of Toronto CSBE, supervised by Prof. Shoshanna Saxe. Conducted literature reviews to identify research gaps in building material use and sustainability. Developed a 3D multi-phase building model in Autodesk Revit, using material take-off tools to calculate material volumes, convert to masses, and assess cradle-to-gate GHG emissions across multiple building intervention scenarios. Performed data analysis in Python. Presented at the 2024 CSBE Embodied GHG Symposium and at ETH Zurich SBE25 (2025). Extended GHG analysis currently under peer review for publication.

🏆 Winner · Climate Hackathon 2024
Toronto Urban Heat Vulnerability & Cooling Centre Optimisation
Led a data-driven Python and QGIS pipeline to identify 8 optimal cooling centre locations for 2.8M Toronto residents. Downscaled CanESM5 climate projections (SSP2-4.5 & SSP5-8.5, 2070–2100) using Quantile Delta Mapping; calculated Humidex heat hazard days; overlaid population vulnerability indices (income, age, housing status, education) to produce ward-level risk maps.

⚡ Energy Systems Policy
Net-Zero Pathway Modelling — The Netherlands 2040
Developed a national decarbonisation scenario for the Netherlands targeting net-zero by 2040 across electricity generation, buildings, and agriculture — representing 59% of national emissions. Modelled grid transition (offshore wind 21.5 GW + 65 GW solar + nuclear), building retrofit programme (8.8M buildings), and agricultural emissions abatement (−53%). Quantified costs and social enabling factors.
Energy Modelling GHG Scenario Excel Policy Analysis
CIV1399 · University of Toronto · Dec 2023
🌱 Comparative LCA
Cradle-to-Grave LCA of Canadian, Mediterranean & EAT-Lancet Diets
Conducted full cradle-to-grave life cycle assessment of three standard diets using OpenLCA (Ecoinvent v3.10), IPCC 2013, AWARE water scarcity model, and the dataFIELD database. Quantified GHG and freshwater stress impacts, performed multi-parameter sensitivity analysis, and modelled national-scale reductions from dietary shifts (~1 MtCO₂e/day potential).
